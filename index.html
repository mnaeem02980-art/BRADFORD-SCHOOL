About Bradford School

Bradford School is a premier educational institution located in Kohat, dedicated to providing quality education and holistic development to its students. With a student body of 450 bright and enthusiastic learners, we pride ourselves on creating an environment that fosters academic excellence and personal growth.

Our mission is to empower students with knowledge, skills, and values that will help them succeed in their academic pursuits and become responsible citizens of society.
